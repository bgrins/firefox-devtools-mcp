What the counter rate covers

The estimate includes depot handling, line-haul between any two Ironvale depots on the same regional loop, and one delivery attempt. Residential delivery, timed windows, and remote-area surcharges are added at booking. Parcels over 32 kg or with any dimension over 200 cm must be tendered as freight.
