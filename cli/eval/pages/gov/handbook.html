Section 1 — Purpose & Authority

The requirements below apply uniformly across divisions unless a written waiver has been issued by the Director.

Failure to comply with this section may result in administrative action ranging from written counseling to revocation of delegated authority, consistent with the progressive framework described in the Enforcement Actions section of this handbook.

Questions concerning interpretation of this section should be directed to the Office of Compliance Counsel before the covered action is taken, not after. Informal guidance is advisory; only written determinations bind the bureau.

Each division maintains a desk procedure implementing this section, tailored to its systems and staffing. Desk procedures must be consistent with this handbook and are subject to review during the annual compliance assessment.

The Committee may issue interpretive bulletins clarifying the application of this section to novel circumstances. Bulletins are numbered, indexed in the appendix, and remain in force until incorporated into a subsequent handbook revision.

Section 2 — Definitions

Compliance with this section is a condition of continued authorization under the bureau charter.

The Committee may issue interpretive bulletins clarifying the application of this section to novel circumstances. Bulletins are numbered, indexed in the appendix, and remain in force until incorporated into a subsequent handbook revision.

Personnel must document each covered action in the designated system of record within five business days, including the basis for the action and the identity of the approving supervisor. Where documentation cannot be completed within that window, an interim memorandum must be filed and reconciled at the next monthly review.

Supervisors are responsible for verifying, on a quarterly basis, that the procedures described here are being followed within their units. Verification results are reported to the Compliance Committee, which may direct corrective measures, additional training, or a targeted audit as circumstances warrant.

Exceptions may be granted only in writing and only where the requesting official demonstrates that strict compliance would materially impair a statutory function. Exception requests are logged, reviewed at the following Committee session, and expire automatically after one review cycle unless renewed.

Failure to comply with this section may result in administrative action ranging from written counseling to revocation of delegated authority, consistent with the progressive framework described in the Enforcement Actions section of this handbook.
