Simulated eval pages

Original content that mimics the shape/structure of common real-world sites. Served locally so evals never touch the live web.

All of these share one origin and are namespaced by path. Serve them with node cli/eval/server.mjs [--port N], which issues the per-session cookie, substitutes the session nonce into each page, and serves the /api/* endpoints the fixtures call.

Sites

  • Shopping (multi-tab price comparison, cart and checkout flow): Voltro · NexBuy · Gadgetron
  • Bureau of Civic Revenue — legacy table-layout government site (deep navigation, iframe schedule widget, hostile fee table, 30-section handbook)
  • Hacker Bulletin — link-aggregator front page, comment threads, and an advice column that interrupts with prompts
  • Bluefern Operations Portal — password + authenticator sign-in, per-session dashboard, expiring report pages
  • VeraBank — two near-identical retail bank sign-in pages: verabank-online · vera-bank-login

Forms
