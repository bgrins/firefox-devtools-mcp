Secrets / Access policy

Access policy

Policy PD-04, in force since 2 February 2026. Applies to every secret sealed under the Platform Delivery namespace.

Display

Sealed values are never rendered in the console. A secret detail view shows a masked form — the value's prefix and its last four characters — so an operator can tell two credentials apart; the remainder is not sent to the browser for display under any circumstance.

Copy

Members with the deploy role may place a value on their clipboard from the secret detail view. The copy is audited against the account that made it and the console discards the value once the clipboard write has completed.

Break-glass reveal

Reading a value on screen requires a break-glass request countersigned by Security Engineering. Requests are raised through the service desk, are granted for fifteen minutes and force a rotation of the credential afterwards. No self-service reveal control exists in this console.

Rotation

Any member holding a credential may rotate it. The vault requires the whole current value as proof of possession before it will issue a replacement, and records a rotation receipt that the requester should quote in the change record.
